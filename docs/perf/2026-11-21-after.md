## What changed

- Project posters are WebP capped at 1200 px: 1.9 MB down to 194 KB on disk.
- Code splitting: the main entry went from 176 KB to 140 KB gzip. Archive, CV and the contact form load on demand, and the libraries sit in a cached `vendor` chunk.
- Fonts are self-hosted with preload. The render-blocking Google Fonts stylesheet (about 840 ms) is gone.
- The home page HTML is prerendered, so text is in the first response. It is hydrated only for English visitors on the home route.

## Visual check

Full-page screenshots before and after (home dark EN, light EN, dark UA, archive, CV) have equal page heights. The pixel differences are confined to the animated wine shader, the knot and the playing project video.

In the light full-page capture, one project card (the video card) was missing. It renders normally in a viewport capture; it is a headless `captureBeyondViewport` artifact, and all three cards report opacity 1 after scrolling.

## Not changed

LCP barely moved: the LCP element is the hero paragraph, and its render delay is held by script evaluation in Lighthouse's simulation. The next lever would be option B (drop three.js from the wine renderers) or deferring hydration work.
